I wrapped up at JPMorgan Chase yesterday after three years on the consumer card frontend. Now I'm looking for what's next.

## What I do

I'm a **product engineer** — frontend-leaning, with three years of React, TypeScript, Redux-Saga, TanStack Query/Table, and a lot of careful work on data-dense, customer-facing UIs at the scale of a major bank. I care about design systems, performance, accessibility, and the small details that make interfaces feel right — and I care about whether we're shipping the right thing, not just shipping it well.

Outside of work, I built [The Beuter Design](https://github.com/nathannewyen/the-beuter-design-ecommerce-platform) — a Next.js 16 / React 19 / Tailwind v4 ecommerce frontend with full i18n, Storybook, and Playwright coverage. It's the stack I want to keep working in.

## What I'm looking for

The shape of team and problem matters more to me than the title. Roughly:

- **Frontend-heavy work** — building real product surfaces, not just glue code.
- **A real design partnership** — engineers who care about the design system and designers who care about what's shippable.
- **A modern stack** — React, TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind, server components. Or a strong reason for the choices you've made instead.
- **Product-minded teammates** — people who ask "is this the right thing to build" before they ask "how do we build it."
- **Atlanta or remote** — open to hybrid for the right team.

I'm open to anything — startup or established company, IC or senior IC, any stage. If you're working on developer tools, design systems, ecommerce, fintech UI, or anything where the frontend is the product, I want to hear about it.
