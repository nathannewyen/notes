Kicked off the new year with an omakase dinner, and honestly? It hit different this time.

![Omakase course](/images/posts/2026-omakase.jpg)

There's something nice about just sitting down and letting the chef decide everything. No menu scrolling, no "what should I get" back and forth. You just show up, trust the process, and enjoy what comes.

## Why it felt right

Last year was a lot. Nonstop building, shipping, grinding. Good stuff, but exhausting. So starting 2026, I just wanted to slow down for a bit. Be present. Actually taste my food instead of inhaling it between meetings.

And that's exactly what omakase gave me.

Each piece came out one at a time. I noticed things I usually miss—how the rice was still warm, how the fish basically melted, the little details in how everything was prepared. It's the kind of meal that makes you put your phone away without even thinking about it.

## A thought for the year

I don't really do resolutions, but sitting at that counter, I kept thinking: maybe I should approach more things this way. Less planning, more trusting. Less controlling, more being present for whatever happens.

We'll see how that goes. But for now—solid start to the year.
